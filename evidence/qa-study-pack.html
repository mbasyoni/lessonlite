Original lesson notes

Evaporation changes liquid water into water vapour. Energy from the Sun helps water evaporate from oceans, lakes and other surfaces. Condensation happens when water vapour cools and becomes tiny liquid droplets. Clouds contain droplets or ice crystals. Precipitation returns water from clouds to the ground as rain, snow or other forms. Water can collect in rivers, seep into the ground, or return to the ocean.
